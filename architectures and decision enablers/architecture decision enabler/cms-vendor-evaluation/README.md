# CMS (Content Management system platform) Vendor Selection Decision Enabler

A structured, automated Excel-based selection calculator built to accelerate your COTS CMS platform procurement journey. This tool helps organizations evaluate vendors.

## 🛠️ Problem 

* 📐 [What?](./images/problem.jpg)
* 📊 [How?](./images/solution.jpg)

## 🚀 Key Features

* **CMS (Content Management system platform) Vendor Assessment:** Evaluates different ESB vendors (Adobe AEM, Drupal, Mongo DB, Optimizely, Sitecore) on the below parameters :

|Parameters|
   |-|
   |**Features & Capabilities**|
   |**Scalability & Performance**|
   |**Integration & Ecosystem**|
   |**Security & Compliance**|
   |**Ease of Use & Admin**|
   |**Community & Support**|
   |**Cost & Licensing**|
   |**Extensibility & Customization**|
   |**Learning Curve**|
   |**Multi-Cloud & Hybrid Support**| 
   |**Analytics & Reporting**|
   |**Skilled Resources (offshore)**|
   |**Content Authoring & Workflow**|
   |**Omnichannel Delivery**|
   |**Data Governance & Privacy**|
