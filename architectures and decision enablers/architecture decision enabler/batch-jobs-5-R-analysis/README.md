# Batch jobs 5-R Macro (pre-TCO) analysis Decision Enabler

A structured, automated Excel-based selection calculator built to accelerate your batch jobs 5-R macro (pre-tco) analysis journey. This tool helps organizations make the decision to replace, refactor, redesign, retire or retain legacy batch jobs.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **6-R Macro (pre-TCO) analysis :** Decides by evaluating through 40 point (15 parameters) whether to migrate an application to cloud or refactor or replace or rearchitect:

|Parameters|
   |-|
   |**Business Value and Functionality**|
   |**Regulatory and Compliance Needs**|
   |**Frequency of Execution**|
   |**Job Complexity**|
   |**Dependency on Legacy Technology**|
   |**Maintenance Costs**|
   |**COTS Availability**|
   |**Risk of Failure**|
   |**Security Concerns**|
   |**Modernization Feasibility**| 
   |**Stakeholder Dependency**|
   |**Resource Availability**|
   |**Integration Complexity**|
   |**Expected Lifecycle**|
   |**Long-term Strategic Alignment**|

|Strategy |Business Explanation|
|-|-|
|**Retire:**| The job is non-essential, rarely used, not critical to operations, with minimal dependencies and low modernization feasibility. |
|**Refactor:**| The job is moderately valuable and has minor inefficiencies. Refactoring can optimize performance, clear technical debt, or improve compatibility with minimal changes. | 
|**Redesign:**| The job is highly critical, handles sensitive customer data, and has a long lifecycle. |
|**Replace with COTS:**| A COTS payroll solution meets all functionality and compliance needs. |
|**Retain AS-IS:**| The job is highly critical, stable, and cost-effective. |
